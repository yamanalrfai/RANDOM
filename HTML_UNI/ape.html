Empowering Parents.

Supporting Children With Autism





Raising a child on the autism spectrum is a unique and powerful journey—one that comes with challenges, breakthroughs, and moments of deep connection. At APE, we believe that every parent has the strength, love, and intuition needed to guide their child toward a meaningful and fulfilling life. Our mission is to walk beside you, offering support, tools, and a community that understands. You are not alone—and together, we can celebrate progress, honor individuality, and create a path forward filled with hope and possibility.

Autism spectrum disorder (ASD) is a complex developmental condition involving persistent challenges with social communication, restricted interests and repetitive behavior. While autism is considered a lifelong condition, the need for services and supports because of these challenges varies among individuals with autism. According to the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention, an estimated one in 36 children has been identified with autism spectrum disorder.
